Furniture Store Delivery and Returns Policy

Section 1. Delivery standards.
All furniture orders are delivered by our two-person delivery team on the
date selected at checkout. Customers must ensure that hallways, doorways,
and stairwells are clear before the team arrives. If a delivery cannot be
completed because access is blocked, a redelivery is scheduled and a
redelivery fee may apply.

Section 2. Stairs and access fees.
Standard delivery includes carrying items up to two flights of stairs at
no extra charge. Deliveries that require carrying items beyond two
flights of stairs without elevator access incur a stairs fee, which is
paid by the customer. The stairs fee is quoted before the delivery date
and collected by the delivery team on arrival. The store covers the
stairs fee only when the product page explicitly offers free walk-up
delivery or when a delivery error on our side caused the extra carry.

Section 3. Mattress returns.
Every mattress comes with a 100-night sleep trial. A customer may return
a mattress even if it has been unwrapped and slept on; original packaging
is not required for mattress returns. Returned mattresses are collected
by our courier partner, inspected, and then donated or recycled whenever
condition allows. Refunds for mattress returns are issued to the original
payment method within ten business days of collection.

Section 4. Other furniture returns.
Furniture other than mattresses may be returned within 30 days of
delivery, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.
Items showing signs of use or missing packaging may be refused or may
incur a restocking fee.

Section 5. Damaged or defective items.
If an item arrives damaged, the customer should contact support within 14
days with photos of the damage. Replacement parts are sent free of charge
when available; otherwise a full replacement or return is arranged.
